SecureLink 2.0

Connect your browser extension

The SecureLink Chrome extension can submit URLs you choose to scan and read the resulting scan findings for one organization. It cannot manage members, domains, cases, or account settings.

Sign in to authorize

Enter your account details on this SecureLink page. Your password is not sent to the extension.

Review access

Choose the organization whose link scans the extension may submit and view.

Access ends after 12 hours. You can disconnect this extension from its popup at any time. Password reset or account deactivation also revokes its access.
